Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026  ·  Applicable law: Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nDSG) and, where applicable, the EU GDPR

1. Who We Are

The controller responsible for this website is Emma's Lab, a handmade craft and candle studio based in Mettmenstetten, Switzerland, operating emmas-lab.ch.

Emma's Lab

2. What Data We Collect

Order requests: when you submit the order form we receive your name, email address, phone number (optional), delivery address (optional), the items you ordered and any notes. We use this only to process your order, communicate with you and arrange delivery.

Contact form: your name, email address and message, used only to respond to your inquiry.

Analytics and advertising data (only with your consent): if you accept cookies in our banner, Google Analytics 4 and the Meta Pixel collect information about your visit: pages viewed, products viewed, items added to the cart, opening of the cart, order requests sent, device and browser information, approximate location (derived from a shortened IP address) and identifiers stored in cookies. For events such as an order request we only send the product names, prices, quantities and cart total. We never send your name, email address, phone number or delivery address to Google or Meta.

Local storage: your shopping cart, language and cookie choice are stored in your browser on your device only and are not sent to us.

3. Purposes and Legal Basis

  • Handling orders and inquiries: necessary to answer your request and perform the contract with you.
  • Analytics (Google Analytics 4): to understand how visitors use the site and improve it. Based on your consent.
  • Advertising (Meta Pixel): to measure the results of our ads on Instagram and Facebook and to show our ads to people who visited our website (retargeting). Based on your consent.

You can decline analytics and advertising cookies in the banner. The website works fully without them.

4. Recipients and Third-Party Services

  • EmailJS (emailjs.com) — delivers the order and contact forms to our email inbox. EmailJS Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd) — usage analytics, only with consent. Google Privacy Policy
  • Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd / Meta Platforms, Inc.) — advertising measurement and retargeting on Facebook and Instagram, only with consent. Meta may use this data as described in its own policy and may combine it with data of users of its platforms. Meta Privacy Policy
  • Delivery partners (for example Swiss Post) receive your name and delivery address when this is necessary to deliver your order.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Transfers Abroad

Google, Meta and EmailJS may process data outside Switzerland, including in the USA. Where this happens we rely on the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework certification of the provider or on standard contractual clauses. Advertising and analytics data is transferred only if you have given your consent.

6. Data Retention

Contact messages are kept for up to 12 months. Order-related emails are kept as long as necessary to fulfil and support your order and to meet Swiss accounting obligations (up to 10 years for accounting records). Google Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. Data held by Meta is subject to Meta's own retention rules.

7. Your Rights

Under the Swiss nDSG and, where applicable, the GDPR you have the right to access the data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to processing, to receive your data in a portable format, and to withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future. You may also lodge a complaint with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or your local supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at emmaslabcandle@gmail.com.

8. Cookies and Withdrawing Consent

We set analytics and advertising cookies only after you accept them in the banner. To withdraw your consent, clear this site's data in your browser (this removes the stored choice and the cookies) and decline in the banner when it appears again. You can also manage ad preferences in your Instagram and Facebook settings. Details are in our Cookie Policy.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss or disclosure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision.

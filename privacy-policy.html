Order requests submitted through our form are transmitted via EmailJS (emailjs.com), a third-party email delivery service. EmailJS processes the data solely to deliver your message to us. Please refer to EmailJS Privacy Policy for details.

5. Local Storage

This website uses your browser's localStorage to save your shopping cart between visits. This data is stored entirely on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser data.

6. Data Retention

Order-related emails are retained in our inbox for as long as necessary to fulfill and support your order, and in compliance with Swiss accounting obligations (up to 10 years for invoices).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at emmaslabcandle@gmail.com.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

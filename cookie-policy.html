Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Applicable law: Swiss nDSG

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to site owners.

2. What We Use

This website does not use cookies. We do not set any tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies.

Instead, we use your browser's localStorage — a similar technology that stores data locally on your device only. It is never sent to our servers and cannot be read by other websites.

3. localStorage We Use

Key Purpose Expires
ecl_cart_v1 Saves your shopping cart items between page visits so you don't lose your selection when you close the browser. Until you clear browser data

4. Third-Party Services

We use EmailJS to transmit order request forms. EmailJS may set its own cookies or storage. Please refer to EmailJS Privacy Policy for details.

5. How to Clear localStorage

You can clear localStorage at any time through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Clear browsing data → Cached images and files
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Clear Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if we introduce new technologies. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026  ·  Applicable law: Swiss FADP

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to site owners.

2. What We Use

Only after you accept cookies in our banner, we load Google Analytics 4, the Google Ads tag and the Meta Pixel, which set analytics and advertising cookies. If you decline, none of these are loaded and no analytics or advertising cookies are set. The website works fully either way.

We also use your browser's localStorage — a similar technology that stores data locally on your device only.

3. Cookies (only with your consent)

_fbpMeta PixelIdentifies the browser to measure the results of our Instagram and Facebook ads and to show our ads to website visitors.3 months

4. localStorage We Use

We use EmailJS to transmit order request forms. EmailJS may set its own cookies or storage. Please refer to EmailJS Privacy Policy for details. More about how your data is processed is in our Privacy Policy.

5. Withdrawing Consent

To withdraw your consent, clear this site's data in your browser (see below). The banner will appear again on your next visit and you can decline. You can also manage ad preferences in your Instagram and Facebook settings.

6. How to Clear Cookies and localStorage

You can clear cookies and localStorage at any time through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Clear browsing data → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Clear Data
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if we introduce new technologies. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

8. Contact

Questions about our cookie practices? Contact us at emmaslabcandle@gmail.com.

1. Что такое cookie?

Cookie — это небольшие текстовые файлы, которые браузер сохраняет на вашем устройстве. Они широко используются, чтобы сайты работали эффективнее и предоставляли информацию их владельцам.

2. Что мы используем

Только после того как вы примете cookie в нашем баннере, мы загружаем Google Analytics 4, тег Google Ads и Meta Pixel, которые устанавливают аналитические и рекламные cookie. Мы также используем localStorage вашего браузера — похожую технологию, которая хранит данные только локально на вашем устройстве.

## 2. Current Positioning (plaster kits)

### Strengths
- Genuinely handmade in Switzerland — real differentiator vs. imported craft kits
- Party Box = unique format, no direct Swiss local competitor found
- 4-language site with multilingual SEO — rare for small artisan shops
- Educational + experiential = ticks the "meaningful gift" box

### Gaps
- No TWINT payment option — 70% of Swiss online buyers expect it
- No social proof / reviews on site
- No testimonials or UGC from real customers
- Etsy presence missing — large Swiss buyer pool shops there

### Direct competitors (kids/party)
- **kinderparties.ch** — party supplies, ships CH, no handmade angle
- **Etsy CH sellers** — imported kits, no Swiss Made story
- **Local craft workshops** (Zurich, Bern, Geneva) — in-person only, higher price point
- **Migros / Manor craft kits** — mass market, no personality

### Positioning gap to own:
> "Swiss handmade party activity — not a toy, not a workshop, something you take home and make together"

## 3. Candle Market — Opportunity Analysis

### Market signals
- Etsy: 288,000+ handmade candle listings globally — highly competitive on generic scented candles
- Winners on Etsy: unique shapes/forms, natural materials (soy/beeswax), beautiful packaging, customization
- Growth driver: Millennials + Gen Z buying candles for mindfulness, home decor, gifting
- Artisanal brands winning on: story + transparency + aesthetics

### Where the gap is
Generic round/pillar scented candles = oversaturated.
**Sculptural / form-based candles** (animals, objects, architectural shapes) = much less competition, higher perceived value, stronger visual on Instagram.

This aligns directly with Emma's existing plaster-casting skill set.

### Candle positioning that works for Emma's Lab

**Option A — "Collector" candles**
Decorative sculptural forms not meant to burn (or burn slowly). Gift item. Higher price point.
Angle: *"too beautiful to burn"*

**Option B — "Craft candle kit"**
Same format as plaster kits — you pour/decorate your own candle. Extends the existing product line logic.
Angle: *"make it together"*, natural progression for existing customers

**Option C — "Swiss home" candles**
Soy or beeswax, Swiss-foraged scents (pine, Alpine herbs), minimal packaging.
Angle: *slow living, local materials, Swiss Made*

**Recommendation: Option B first, then A**
Option B requires the least new inventory/tooling (same kit format, same customer, same party use case), proves demand, then layer in Option A as premium.
